Baa Baa Match 隐私政策

本政策适用于 Baa Baa Match 的 Android 游戏，中文商店名为“消不停”，游戏内也使用“咩咩消消乐／咩咩消消樂”。游戏开发者的支持和隐私联系方式为 l545115382@gmail.com。

下述应用内广告年龄与同意控制适用于设置中提供“广告年龄设置”的更新版本。尚无该入口的旧版本可能在启动时初始化和预加载广告；你可以先使用 Android／Google 的广告控制，安装含上述入口的更新后再使用游戏内控制。

1. 本地存档与年龄组

关卡进度、当前牌局、游戏内货币、道具、已解锁外观、奖励领取记录、语言和声音等设置保存在设备本地。游戏没有自建账户注册系统。Android 系统备份或设备还原可能影响本地数据；本地进度不等于 Google Play 游戏排行榜数据，也不代表已同步到云端存档。

本游戏面向13岁及以上玩家。提供广告年龄设置的版本仅在本机保存“未满13岁、13–17岁、18岁及以上、未选择”这类年龄组，不要求生日或精确年龄。该年龄组被排除在应用配置的系统云备份和设备转移之外，用于广告限制，并可随时在设置中更正。我们不收集或上传生日、精确年龄；可请求广告的年龄组对应的未达同意年龄及广告年龄限制标记会传给 Google。

2. Google Play 游戏与 Drive 授权

排行榜使用已有的 Google Play 游戏玩家资料。Google Play Games Services 会处理玩家名称或昵称、玩家标识符、登录状态，以及服务互动和诊断数据，用于身份验证、账户管理和排行榜。游戏在已登录时可提交最高已通关关卡号；打开排行榜时也会尝试同步已取得的该成绩。昵称、头像及成绩的展示由 Google Play 游戏和玩家资料可见性设置决定。本地游玩不以完成 Google 交互授权为前提；未授权或登录失败时排行榜可能不可用。

Google 授权界面可能包含 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata（“查看、创建和删除其在 Google 云端硬盘中的配置数据”）。此权限的范围是本应用在 Google Drive 中的专属隐藏应用数据文件夹，不授予读取普通 Drive 文档、照片或其他应用隐藏数据的权限。当前游戏未启用 Google 云端存档，也未调用 Drive／Saved Games 接口读取、写入或删除该文件夹，未通过该权限上传本地游戏进度。允许此权限不代表本游戏已使用它；排行榜成绩由 Play 游戏服务管理。

3. 广告、同意与设备数据

游戏集成 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob），包括玩家主动选择观看以获得游戏内奖励的激励广告。提供广告年龄设置的版本采用以下规则：

  • 未选择年龄或未满13岁：不启动广告同意流程、不初始化广告 SDK，也不请求或预加载广告；游戏仍可游玩。这一限制只针对广告，不代表资源更新或 Google Play 游戏等其他服务停止联网。
  • 13–17岁：向 Google 标记未达同意年龄，使用青少年广告限制、最高 G 级广告内容及非个性化广告请求。
  • 18岁及以上：根据 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）判断的适用地区和要求展示同意消息。
  • 对可请求广告的年龄组，每次应用启动先更新同意状态，完成本次更新和必要表单后，才允许初始化、预加载或展示广告。流程失败时关闭本轮广告请求，可通过“设置 → 广告年龄设置”重新选择年龄组来重试。

为确定适用的同意要求、展示表单和记录选择，Google 同意服务可能处理 IP 地址、设备及应用信息和同意选择，并在设备上保存同意记录。广告未展示不等于所有服务均未处理数据。当广告请求被允许后，预加载也可能在你点击观看前发生。Google 广告服务会处理以下数据：

数据用途与接收方
广告标识符、应用实例及其他设备或账户标识符由 Google 广告服务处理，用于广告投放、衡量和分析、反欺诈、安全与合规。
IP 地址及由其推断的大致位置用于广告投放、分析及反欺诈；由网络地址推断大致位置不需要精确定位权限。
应用互动，例如启动、广告点击与视频观看；诊断信息，例如启动时间、卡顿和 SDK 性能用于广告及服务运行、效果衡量和分析、故障诊断、反欺诈、安全与合规。

数据会传送给 Google，并可能由 Google 按其政策与广告合作伙伴共享。不同意个性化用途后，Google 仍可能按适用规则提供有限广告；“允许请求广告”不等于同意个性化广告。非个性化或有限广告也不等于零数据处理。

4. 网络、外部分享与安全

游戏会向资源更新服务器请求版本和资源，服务器会收到 IP 地址及正常网络请求信息。当前资源更新地址使用 HTTP，因此不能保证所有游戏网络传输均经过加密。Google 服务按各自的安全和数据处理政策运行。

如你选择 Facebook 邀请分享，游戏会在外部浏览器或应用中打开 Facebook 分享页，传入本游戏的公开 Google Play 商店链接；此分享链接不附带你的玩家 ID、存档或关卡成绩。Facebook 是否发布内容由你在其页面上的操作决定，游戏不自动代你发布。Facebook 及浏览器可能按各自政策处理访问、登录、Cookie 等数据。

5. 你的选择、授权撤回与数据删除

广告与年龄选择

  • 提供相关入口的版本中，可在“设置 → 广告年龄设置”更正年龄组；选择“不提供”会关闭广告。更改年龄或重新打开广告隐私表单时，游戏停止新广告请求并使已缓存广告失效。
  • Google 要求提供隐私选项时，设置中显示“广告隐私选择”。你可以打开它，拒绝、管理或更改此前的同意；未显示该入口可能表示当前位置／状态下 Google 未要求该入口。撤回不会自动删除已经处理的数据，也不会影响撤回前已经发生的处理。
  • 你也可在 Android／Google 账户设置中管理广告标识符和广告个性化，参阅 Google 我的广告中心。选项随系统、地区和账户变化；重置或删除广告 ID 不会删除所有既有服务数据，也不会必然停止广告。

Google 授权与排行榜

  • 撤回授权：打开 Google 账户的第三方连接，找到本游戏，查看其访问权限，并按 Google 显示的选项移除访问权限或连接。Google Play 游戏设置也可管理用于游戏登录的账户。撤回可能使排行榜等功能不可用；以后再次授权可能恢复服务。移除访问权限与删除已有数据是不同操作。
  • 删除 Google Play 游戏数据：前往 Google Play 游戏玩家资料，在“你的数据／删除资料和游戏数据”中选择删除本游戏的单项数据；也可按 Google 的删除说明从 Play 商店／Play 游戏设置进入。菜单名称可能变化。此操作由 Google 执行，不会清除本机存档或所有广告数据；若保持授权并继续游玩或打开排行榜，本地保留的已通关成绩可能再次提交。为停止后续提交，可同时移除游戏访问权限。
  • Drive 隐藏应用数据：本游戏目前未通过 drive.appdata 写入数据。若 Google Drive 中存在本游戏的隐藏应用数据，可在网页版 Drive 的“设置 → 管理应用”中查找本游戏，并在 Google 提供该选项时删除隐藏应用数据；是否有该项取决于实际存在的数据。不要将撤销授权或卸载手机游戏理解为已删除 Drive 中的所有相关数据。

Baa Baa Match Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026 · Android package: com.ddgame.yangleyang

This policy covers the Android game Baa Baa Match, also named 消不停 in the store and 咩咩消消乐／咩咩消消樂 in the game. Contact the game developer for support or privacy questions at l545115382@gmail.com.

The in-game advertising age and consent controls described below apply to updated versions with “Ad age settings” in Settings. Older versions without that entry may initialize and preload ads at startup. You can use Android and Google advertising controls and use the in-game controls after installing an update that provides them.

1. Local saves and age group

Campaign progress, the current board, in-game currency, items, unlocked cosmetics, reward-claim records, language, sound and other settings are stored locally. The game has no account-registration system of its own. Android backup or device restore may affect local data. Local progress is separate from Google Play Games leaderboard data and does not mean that a cloud save exists.

The game is intended for players aged 13 or older. Versions with ad age settings store only an age group on the device: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or not provided. They do not request a date of birth or exact age. This age-group preference is excluded from the app’s configured system cloud backup and device transfer. It controls advertising restrictions and can be corrected in Settings. We do not collect or upload birthdays or exact ages. Under-age-of-consent and advertising age-restriction flags corresponding to an eligible age group are sent to Google.

2. Google Play Games and Drive permission

Leaderboards use an existing Google Play Games player profile. Google Play Games Services processes player names or nicknames, player identifiers, sign-in status, and service interactions and diagnostics for authentication, account management and leaderboards. When signed in, the game may submit the highest campaign level completed; opening the leaderboard also attempts to synchronize that previously earned score. Google Play Games and profile visibility settings control the display of names, profile images and scores. Local play does not require completing interactive Google authorization. Leaderboards may be unavailable if authorization or sign-in fails.

The Google authorization screen may include https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (“See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive”). This scope covers this app’s own hidden application-data folder. It does not grant access to ordinary Drive documents, photos or other apps’ hidden data. The current game does not enable Google cloud saves or call Drive or Saved Games APIs to read, write or delete this folder, and does not upload local game progress through this permission. Granting a permission does not mean the game currently uses it. Play Games manages leaderboard scores.

3. Advertising, consent and device data

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including rewarded ads that players choose to watch for in-game rewards. Versions with ad age settings use these rules:

  • No age selected or under 13: no advertising consent flow, advertising SDK initialization, ad requests or preloads. The game remains playable. This restriction concerns advertising; resource updates, Google Play Games and other services may still connect to the network.
  • Ages 13–17: Google receives an under-age-of-consent flag; ad requests use teen restrictions, a maximum G content rating and non-personalized advertising.
  • Age 18 or older: Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) determines whether a regional consent message is required.
  • For eligible age groups, each app launch updates consent information. Ad initialization, preloading and display are allowed only after that update and any required forms finish. A failed flow blocks ad requests for that session. Retry through Settings → Ad age settings by selecting the age group again.

To determine consent requirements, show forms and record choices, Google’s consent service may process IP addresses, device and app information and consent choices, and store consent records on the device. An ad not being shown does not mean that no service processes data. Once ad requests are allowed, preloading may occur before you choose to watch. Google advertising services process:

DataPurpose and recipient
Advertising, app-instance and other device or account identifiersProcessed by Google advertising services for ad delivery, measurement and analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance.
IP addresses and approximate location inferred from themAd delivery, analytics and fraud prevention. Inferring approximate location from a network address does not require precise-location permission.
App interactions such as launches, ad clicks and video views; diagnostics such as launch time, slow frames and SDK performanceAdvertising and service operation, measurement and analytics, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, security and compliance.

Data are sent to Google and may be shared by Google with advertising partners under its policies. After you decline personalized purposes, Google may still serve limited ads under applicable rules. Permission to request ads is not consent to personalized advertising. Non-personalized or limited ads do not mean zero data processing.

4. Network requests, external sharing and security

The game requests versions and resources from a resource-update server, which receives IP addresses and normal request information. The current resource-update address uses HTTP, so not all game network traffic can be represented as encrypted. Google services operate under their own security and data-processing policies.

If you choose the Facebook invitation feature, the game opens Facebook’s sharing page in an external browser or app with the game’s public Google Play store link. This share link does not include your player ID, save or level score. You control whether content is published on Facebook’s page; the game does not publish it automatically. Facebook and the browser may process visits, sign-in information and cookies under their policies.

5. Your choices, revocation and deletion

Advertising and age choices

  • In versions providing the entry, correct your age group at Settings → Ad age settings. Choosing “Prefer not to say” turns ads off. Changing age or reopening the privacy form stops new ad requests and invalidates cached ads.
  • “Ad privacy choices” appears in Settings when Google requires that entry. Open it to decline, manage or change earlier consent. Its absence may mean that Google does not require the entry for your current region or state. Withdrawal does not automatically delete data already processed or affect processing that already occurred.
  • Manage advertising identifiers and personalization in Android or Google Account settings, including Google My Ad Center. Options vary by system, region and account. Resetting or deleting an ad ID does not erase all previous service data or necessarily stop ads.

Google authorization and leaderboards

  • Revoke access: open Google Account third-party connections, find this game, review access and use Google’s options to remove access or the connection. Google Play Games settings also manage the account used for game sign-in. Revocation may make leaderboards unavailable; authorizing again may restore service. Removing access and deleting existing data are separate actions.
  • Delete Google Play Games data: open your Google Play Games profile and use Your Data / Delete profile and game data to delete this game’s individual data. You can also follow Google’s deletion instructions through Play Store or Play Games settings; labels may change. Google performs this deletion. It does not clear local saves or all advertising data. If authorization remains and you continue playing or open the leaderboard, a completed score retained locally may be submitted again. Remove game access as well to stop later submissions.
  • Drive hidden app data: this game does not currently write data using drive.appdata. If hidden data for the game exists in Drive, look for the game under Settings → Manage apps in Drive on the web and delete hidden app data if Google offers that option. Availability depends on data actually existing. Revoking access or uninstalling the phone game does not mean that all related Drive data has been deleted.

Local data and contacting us

  • Clear local data: in Android Settings → Apps → this game → Storage, choose Clear data / Clear storage. This removes local progress, currency, cosmetics and settings. Clearing only the cache does not clear the save. Uninstalling usually removes local app data, but system backup or restore may restore some data. Clearing local data does not delete Google service data. Confirm that you accept losing local progress before doing this.
  • Support, privacy or deletion inquiries: email l545115382@gmail.com with the game name, your question and the data concerned. Do not send passwords, verification codes or complete identity documents. We will review data within the developer’s control and help you find the relevant controls. We cannot directly operate your Google Account or erase all data managed by Google. The game has no account system of its own and no in-game button that deletes all third-party data.

Google retains and deletes Play Games, consent and advertising data under its relevant policies, purposes and account controls; we do not promise a single retention period for Google services. Messages sent to the support email are used to handle your inquiry. To request deletion of email content, contact the same address; we will review what can be handled and any applicable retention requirements.
