Baa Baa Match Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026 · Android package: com.ddgame.yangleyang

This policy describes data handling in the Baa Baa Match Android game, also named 消不停, and its integrated services. Contact the game developer for support or privacy questions at l545115382@gmail.com.

1. Local saves and Google Play Games

Campaign progress, the current game state, in-game currency, items and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not offer its own account registration. Leaderboards use your existing Google Play Games player profile.

Google Play Games Services processes player names or nicknames, player identifiers, sign-in status, and interactions and diagnostics related to game services for authentication, account management and leaderboard functionality. When signed in, the game may submit the highest campaign level you have completed. Opening the leaderboard also attempts to synchronize that previously earned score. Google processes and stores leaderboard scores. The display of your nickname, profile image and scores depends on Google Play Games and your profile visibility settings.

2. Advertising and device data

The game integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including rewarded ads. The SDK initializes and preloads ads when the game starts. Advertising-related processing may therefore occur even if you do not tap or watch a rewarded ad.

Google advertising services process advertising identifiers, app-instance and other device or account identifiers; IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them; app interactions such as launches, ad clicks and video views; and diagnostic information such as launch time, slow frames and other SDK performance information. These data support advertising, measurement and analytics, service operation and diagnostics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Approximate location may be inferred from a network address without precise-location permission.

These data are transmitted to Google. Google advertising services may share data with advertising partners under their policies. Advertising personalization and controls also depend on your Google, Android and advertising-service settings. Non-personalized ads can still use identifiers and diagnostic data.

3. Network requests, retention and security

The game checks a resource-update server and requests game resources. The server receives the IP address and request information normally included in network requests. The current resource-update address uses HTTP, so not all game network traffic can be represented as encrypted. Google services operate under their respective security and data-processing policies.

Local saves remain on your device until overwritten by the game or removed through actions such as clearing app data or uninstalling. System backup and restore may also affect local data. Retention and deletion of Google Play Games scores and advertising-service data are governed by the relevant Google services, policies and settings. We do not specify a single retention period for Google services.

4. Your choices and deletion

  • Use Android app settings to clear this game's local data. This does not also delete scores or advertising data stored by Google services.
  • Manage your player profile, visibility and deletion of this game's Play Games data in Google Play Games settings. See Google's instructions for deleting your profile and game data. Signing in and playing again after deletion may create new data.
  • Manage advertising identifiers and ad personalization in Android and Google Account settings. Available options vary by Android version and region. Resetting or deleting an advertising identifier does not delete all previously stored service data.
  • For questions about this game's data handling, email l545115382@gmail.com. Google provides privacy controls for your Google Account and its service data.

5. Third-party policies and updates

Google's processing is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Further information is available in the Google Play Games data-disclosure guide and Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure guide. These services have their own policies and controls.
